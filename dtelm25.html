Events · DTELM 2025

KAUST Workshop on Distributed Training in the Era of Large Models

24–26 November 2025 · KAUST, Thuwal, Saudi Arabia
Building 4 & 5, Level 0, Auditorium 0215
Original KAUST page (may be archived)

A three-day in-person workshop on scaling learning algorithms to the computational and data demands of modern AI models: distributed optimization, communication-efficient algorithms, model-parallel architectures, and hardware–software co-design. Invited talks, lightning talks, a poster session, and informal discussion.
