Optimization and Big Data 2013

Workshop, trek and colloquium · 1–3 May 2013 · Edinburgh, Scotland
Informatics Forum G.07 (workshop) and JCMB (colloquium), University of Edinburgh
Organizer: Peter Richtárik
Headline speaker: Steve Wright (Wisconsin–Madison)
Original Edinburgh page · full copy

A workshop on optimization algorithms and codes that scale to problems with millions or billions of variables, in machine learning, compressed sensing, social network science, and computational biology.

Best poster prize (sponsored by SAS): Tuomo Valkonen (Cambridge), Computational problems in magnetic resonance imaging. Jury: Steve Wright and Imre Pólik.

Speakers

  • Shai Shalev-Shwartz (Jerusalem) — Stochastic dual coordinate ascent methods for regularized loss minimization · slides
  • Anatoli Juditsky (Joseph Fourier) — Low iteration cost algorithms for large-scale nonsmooth learning problems · slides
  • Peter Richtárik (Edinburgh) — Mini-batch primal and dual methods for support vector machines · slides
  • Imre Pólik (SAS Institute) — Big data optimization at SAS · slides
  • Olivier Fercoq (Edinburgh) — Smooth minimization of nonsmooth functions by parallel coordinate descent · slides
  • Zaid Harchaoui (INRIA) — Large-scale learning with conditional gradient algorithms
  • Richard Samworth (Cambridge) — Optimisation challenges in modern statistics · slides
  • Steve Wright (Wisconsin–Madison) — Randomized optimization algorithms for learning · slides
  • Martin Takáč (Edinburgh) — Alternating maximization: unifying framework for 8 sparse PCA formulations · slides
  • Martin Jaggi (École Polytechnique) — Frank-Wolfe optimization with applications to structured prediction · slides
  • Jacek Gondzio (Edinburgh) — Second order methods for L1-regularization · slides
  • Robert Freund (MIT) — The Frank-Wolfe algorithm: new results, connections to statistical boosting, and computation · slides
  • Des Higham (Strathclyde) — Trick or Tweet?
